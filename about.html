I'm Rakesh, a photographer based in Bengaluru, India.

I enjoy photographing people as they are — the expressions between poses, the quiet moments between celebrations, and the personality that makes every person different.

My work currently focuses on model portraits, pre-wedding stories and children. I keep the process relaxed and natural, while still paying attention to light, composition and the details that make a photograph feel like yours.
